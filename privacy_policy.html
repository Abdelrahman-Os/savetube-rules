Privacy Policy for SaveTube

Effective Date: November 2026

SaveTube ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share your personal files, downloaded videos, audio files, or private vault media on our servers. All downloaded media and vault files remain strictly on your device.

2. Permissions Used

  • Storage / Media Access: Used only to save downloaded videos/audio and display WhatsApp statuses you choose to save.
  • Biometric (Fingerprint / Face): Handled locally by your Android device to unlock your Private Vault. We never access or store your biometric data.
  • Notifications: Used to show download progress and completion status.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services which may collect anonymous usage data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying ads in the free version.
  • Google Play Billing: For processing ad-free subscriptions securely via Google Play.

4. Copyright & Compliance

SaveTube is a web browser and personal media manager. Users are solely responsible for respecting copyright laws. Downloading from YouTube is not supported in compliance with Google Play policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Google Play Store developer email.

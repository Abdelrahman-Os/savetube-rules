Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Our app does not collect, store, or share your personal files, downloaded videos, audio files, or private vault media on our servers. All downloaded media and vault files remain strictly on your device.

2. Permissions Used

  • Storage / Media Access: Used by our app only to save downloaded videos/audio and display WhatsApp statuses you choose to save.
  • Biometric (Fingerprint / Face): Handled locally by your Android device to unlock your Private Vault. Our app never accesses or stores your biometric data.
  • Notifications: Used by our app to show download progress and completion status.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services which may collect anonymous usage data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: For anonymous app usage statistics and performance insights to improve app stability.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying ads in the free version of our app.
  • Google Play Billing: For processing ad-free subscriptions securely via Google Play.

4. Copyright & Compliance

Our app is a web browser and personal media manager. Users are solely responsible for respecting copyright laws. Downloading from YouTube is not supported in compliance with Google Play policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Google Play Store developer email.
